Dear sir,

Short, and not in the usual register.

I have opened an account and started trading. Crypto, small amounts, nothing I
cannot lose. I am telling you because I have told you everything else and it
would be strange to begin withholding at the first thing that is merely
practical.

There is no argument in this letter. That is what is notable about it. Every
other thing I have brought you in two years I have brought because I could not
put it down — the tubules, the monopoles, the mountain, all of it held because
holding was involuntary. This I have simply decided.

The fees are due in March. My mother has not asked and will not ask. The lecture
series is not happening. I have looked at the actual arithmetic of the next
eighteen months and found that it does not work, and that no amount of being
right about Dirac adjusts it.

I notice I have written four paragraphs justifying a decision I said had no
argument in it, which tells you something about how comfortable I am.

Yours sincerely, Morayo
