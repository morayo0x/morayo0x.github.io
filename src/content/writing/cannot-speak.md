Dear sir,

This is the last thing I have to say on the subject I have been circling for a
year, and I say it knowing that stopping is not the same as finishing.

The question was whether the two selves could be one. I have tried it as a
problem of honesty, and as a problem of scale, and as a problem of audience, and
each framing worked for a few weeks and then quietly failed, and the failures
had a family resemblance I could not name until recently.

They failed because each framing required me to _state_ the thing that the two
selves are supposed to share. And I begin to suspect that the thing cannot be
stated — not because it is mystical, but because it is the condition under which
my stating anything makes sense, and a condition cannot be one of the items it
conditions.

You will see where this is going. Whereof one cannot speak, thereof one must be
silent.

I am aware that quoting him here is a manoeuvre. It lets me put down a year of
enquiry with an air of having concluded something, when what I have actually
done is found a respectable sentence to stop at. A proposition that cannot be
stated and a proposition that I have failed to state look identical from where I
am standing, and I have no test that distinguishes them.

So take this as a report of where I got to, not as a result. The two selves have
not been reconciled. They have been assigned a common boundary that I cannot
inspect, which is a different thing, and possibly a worse one.

Yours sincerely, Morayo
