Dear sir,

The room is not coming. I have stopped waiting for the reply and started working
out what the silence means, which is a poorer kind of enquiry but the only one
available.

Williams was the one who said yes. Not enthusiastically — he said the Thursday
slot was free and that we should put something in writing, and I put something
in writing, and that was in March. The follow-up in May was acknowledged. The
one in June was not.

I have considered the possibilities in order of how much they flatter me, and
discarded them in the same order. That he disliked the proposal: unlikely, since
he improved it unprompted. That the department refused: possible, but a refusal
is a cheaper thing to send than silence. That we are too junior to chase: true,
and not an explanation, since he knew that in March.

What remains is that he is no longer there. It explains everything the other
theories explain and also explains the shape of the silence, which began
mid-correspondence rather than at a decision point.

I record this because I notice I spent four months assuming the obstacle was the
_idea_, and revising it accordingly — three drafts, each more modest than the
last. The idea was never being read. The lesson is not about lecture series. It
is that when a thing stalls I look first for a fault in the thing, and an
institution is not a thing with faults, it is a set of rooms with people in
them, and people leave.

Yours sincerely, Morayo
